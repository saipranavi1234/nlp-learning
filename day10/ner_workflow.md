# Named Entity Recognition (NER)

## Definition

Named Entity Recognition (NER) is an NLP technique used to identify and classify named entities in unstructured text into predefined categories. These categories commonly include person names, organizations, locations, dates, times, currencies, and percentages.

NER helps computers understand important information contained in text by recognizing meaningful words or phrases.

## Types of Named Entities

- Person
- Organization
- Location
- Date
- Time
- Money
- Percentage
- Product
- Event

## How NER Works

The text is first preprocessed through tokenization and cleaning. A trained NER model then analyzes each word and predicts whether it belongs to an entity. Finally, each identified entity is assigned a category such as Person or Organization.

## Advantages

- Extracts important information automatically.
- Improves search and document analysis.
- Supports many NLP applications.
- Reduces manual data processing.

## Limitations

- May fail on ambiguous names.
- Performance depends on training data.
- Difficult for domain-specific entities.

## Applications

NER is widely used in search engines, chatbots, healthcare, finance, legal document analysis, recommendation systems, and information extraction.
